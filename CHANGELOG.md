### 5. Case Studies (Vaka Çalışmaları)

Tüm 3 case study tamamen yenilendi:

#### Case Study 1: PCF of Semi-Finished Goods
**Önceki Başlık:** "PCF for Operational Efficiency in Semi-Finished Goods"  
**Yeni Başlık:** "PCF of Semi-Finished Goods"

**Context:** 
ISO 14067-aligned PCF for 11 semi-finished metal products for OEM compliance and decarbonization opportunities.

**Key Findings:**
- 8-week delivery with verified PCFs
- Identified inefficient energy recovery system hotspot
- High-fidelity results from mass-balancing and secondary datasets

**Impact:**
- Measurable carbon reduction
- Energy savings and lower operating costs
- First-pass OEM acceptance (zero corrective actions)
- Competitive advantage vs. market competitors

---

#### Case Study 2: Scope 3 Accounting and Supplier Engagement
**TAMAMEN YENİ - ESG Risk Mitigation değiştirildi**

**Context:**
Global manufacturing company committed to SBTi but >80% of emissions in Scope 3 (supply chain). Spend-based estimates masking critical hotspots.

**Project & Approach:**
- **Screening:** Materiality assessment for top suppliers (70% of emissions)
- **Engagement:** Moved from spend-based to physical intensity data (kgCO2e/kg)
- **Capacity Building:** GHG accounting workshops for suppliers

**Results:**
- Actual emissions 22% different than estimates
- Validated near-term SBTi targets
- 65% of strategic suppliers committed to renewable energy targets within 24 months
- Long-term transition plan de-risked

---

#### Case Study 3: Critical Raw Materials Analysis
**TAMAMEN YENİ - LCA-Driven Design Change değiştirildi**

**Başlık:** "Critical Raw Materials Analysis for Gallium & Germanium"

**Context:**
High dependency on Gallium & Germanium for semiconductors/optical products. >90% supply concentration in China with active export controls.

**Project:**
- Deep-dive market analysis of single-source risks
- "China Plus One" multisourcing strategy
- Validated secondary supply streams via high-purity recycling

**Impact:**
- Mitigated immediate supply disruption risks
- Avoided >40% spot market price surges
- Secured long-term critical mineral regulation compliance
